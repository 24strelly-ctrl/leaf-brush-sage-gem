### What are AI character prompts?
AI character prompts are detailed descriptions designed to generate unique, compelling characters using AI image generation tools like Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion. Each prompt includes visual descriptions, personality traits, backstories, and specialized attributes.

### What do I get with my purchase?
You receive a comprehensive prompt package including:
